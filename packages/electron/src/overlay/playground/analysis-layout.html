My picks:
  • L1 (info row) — most discoverable, keeps the board clean, easy to wire. Both depth and flip make sense as labeled chips.
  • H2 (merged bar) — addresses your "too many bars" concern directly and makes the result feel calmer. Tabs and window controls coexist fine on one row.
L2 (board overlay) is more "modern" but the badges fight with arrows / pieces near the corners. L3 ties depth to the eval bar nicely but separates orientation from the board it controls. L4 adds a row.
